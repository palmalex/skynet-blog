I'm palmalex. Idle Cycle is where I write down what I build and think about when the CPU of my day job is idle: side projects, systems, hardware and ideas that seemed worth keeping.

Much of it happens in a home lab: a small Proxmox host, a couple of Raspberry Pis and a fair amount of infrastructure as code, because I like being able to rebuild whatever I break.

This site is static, built from a public repository and published to Cloudflare R2. How it got that way is [the first post](@/blog/zola-on-r2/index.md).
